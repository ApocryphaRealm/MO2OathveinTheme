# Third-party notices - Oathvein MO2 Theme

No third-party files are included.

## The UI mod this theme sits beside

* **Oathvein UI** by Nithog (https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/160916) - its colours and shape language, through the AMF "Oathvein" theme. No files of it are used; it is not required.

## Apocrypha Menu Framework, Njordlinger MO2 Theme

The frame and background art (`src\amf-art\`) is Apocrypha Menu Framework's, and the icons (`src\icons\`) are the Njordlinger MO2 Theme's, recoloured - both by the same author (ApocryphaRealm, GPL-3.0-or-later).

## Mod Organizer 2

Widget selectors and the toolbar action names come from Mod Organizer 2 (https://github.com/ModOrganizer2/modorganizer, GPL-3.0).
