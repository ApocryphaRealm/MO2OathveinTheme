# Changelog - Oathvein MO2 Theme

Versions are issued by the project's version gate. Written as the change happens (rule 61).

## 1.0.0 - 2026-09-26

First version. The owner: *"now make themes from scratch for norden, norden black, oathvein, veldun, and untarnished ui"*, *"refer to the amf themes they are based on"*, *"you can keep the icons but recolor them to fit the themes"*, *"we need to scale up the corner art to make it more prominent"*, *"package them separatly"*.

* A stylesheet written for these themes with the AMF "Oathvein" theme's palette and graded tints: charcoal panels, thin grey lines with crossed scratch marks at the corners, a blood-red highlight and dark red separators.
* The AMF theme's frame and background art, at full size around group boxes and tab panes.
* Buttons, check boxes, radio buttons and arrows drawn in the theme's shape.
* Separators and mods coloured separately; MO2's conflict highlight kept.
* The Njordlinger icon set, recoloured to the theme's line colour.
